The MKL82 debug sequence can now connect to a target that is asleep in a stop mode (STOP, VLPS, LLS, VLLS) or reset-looping. When the AHB-AP does not respond, it resets the system through the MDM-AP and halts the core at the reset vector, and connect-under-reset uses the MDM-AP as well, so `run`, `attach`, `reset`, `erase` and `info` no longer fail with a WAIT response against a sleeping chip.
